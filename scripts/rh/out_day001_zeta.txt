== S3c the leak test: break the mirror, watch a zero leave the line ==
  beta(s) = L(s, chi_-4), primitive conductor 4 = 2^2.
  'Leak': lift it to modulus 12 = 2*3 (a non-prime-power, composite
  conductor):  L12(s) = (1 + 2^{-s}) beta(s).
  the lifted Gauss sum:  |tau_phi|^2 = 4.0000   vs modulus 12  (ratio 0.333)
  -> the completed L-function of the leak is NOT self-reciprocal
  (a primitive mirror has |tau|^2 = conductor = 4 for chi_-4).
  the factor (1+2^(-0.95-400j)) zeroes at s = i pi/ln2 = 4.532360142 i   (Re = 0)
  |L12( i pi/ln2 )|  = 5.359e-16   <- ZERO, off the 1/2 line
  |L12( 1- conj(s) )| = 5.693e-01   <- mirror slot EMPTY (|beta| 1.1385, |factor| 0.5000 -- neither vanishes)
  quad symmetry of a genuine zero (gamma1), |zeta| at the four mirror points:
    rho          0.50 +   14.134725 i    |zeta| = 3.404e-15
    conj rho     0.50 +  -14.134725 i    |zeta| = 3.404e-15
    1-rho        0.50 +  -14.134725 i    |zeta| = 3.404e-15
    1-conj rho   0.50 +   14.134725 i    |zeta| = 3.404e-15
  verdict: leak the mirror (non-primitive conductor) and a zero
  appears OFF the line, paired only with its conjugate, its mirror

  self-reciprocal (|Gauss sum|^2 = 4, not the modulus 12).  This is

  It is a theorem-level fact, not a proof of RH: it localizes the
