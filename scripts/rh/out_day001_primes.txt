[Q3] 1/2 for primes: halving, the 2-adic splitting depth
  the brief's example: 7 = (7-1)/2 + (7+1)/2 = 3+1/2 + 3+1/2
  for every odd prime p:  p = (p-1)/2 + (p+1)/2, both integers,

  the SPLITTING DEPTH v2(p-1) = how many even halvings p-1 admits:
