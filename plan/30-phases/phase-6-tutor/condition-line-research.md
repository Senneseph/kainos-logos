## D3 — What the band teaches (the teachable condition line)

S12 DECLARED the condition, its closers, and the queue. The
condition line TEACHES it: the infant learns to turn the assumption
ON and reason under it, each consequence keeping its marker, the
line ending where the condition remains open. The four
relationships (R1–R4):

- **R1 — the hypothesis is a premise you may assume, never a fact
  you may hold:** the open conjecture becomes a usable
  assumption — "assume every nontrivial zero of zeta has real part
  exactly one half, then …" — while the conjecture itself stays
  the status surface: believed, not known, assumed in the lot and
  never proved by it. (New concept `hypothesis`.)
- **R2 — the marker travels:** every consequence of the lot carries
  the exact marker "under the condition of the Riemann
  hypothesis"; "a conditional lot read without its condition is a
  misread this store punishes" (the `conditional-def` discipline,
  now drilled rather than only declared).
- **R3 — the three consequences are ONE line:** error of the prime
  count → root scale; gap between consecutive primes → the same
  root scale; running deviation of (−1)^{ω} → root smallness →
  prime-parts uniqueness on the condition road. The line's own
  census stands under it: 20 of the 22 gaps covered by the bare
  scale, failing at exactly the two small-prime witnesses (2, 3);
  the μ-ledger's greatest absolute 4 below the root of the line
  end; the two roads (scan / condition) reach the same destination
  and the condition buys nothing at the destination. (New concept
  `condition-line`.)
- **R4 — the filing fills the line, not the condition:** the filed
  composition names the line (the consequences under the
  assumption, the marker in every clause) and holds the condition
  open — the LOT-1 instance of W-S12-4. (New theorem
  `condition-line-filed`.)

The Riemann island's analytic edge is thereby strengthened as its
status line promised: the queue's declared conditions become
ON-LINE, TEACHABLE facts — while the condition itself stays open
of record.
