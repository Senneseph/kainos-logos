## 0. The owner's principle (as directed, formalized)

> "Revisit fundamental representations, and how they were created,
> only making sure to update with modern techniques. Start where we
> are, and fan out to get the lower level of what we already know.
> Cross-pollinate: start training on slightly related fields,
> building fundamentals backwards in the same way. If we ever reach
> a fundamental level that alters or adds to the layer above it
> during testing, that is a breakthrough. We want to keep track of
> those."

- **P1 — Backward fan-out (down, not sideways).** Every band builds
  the LAYER BENEATH a fact the store already holds: "how was this
  created?" The seed frontier is the S10 store; the first downward
  edge runs from {prime sequence, Euler product, ζ} to its provenance
  layer: divisibility, composites, the sieve process, factorization.
  We do not pick new topics upward; we dig the layer below what
  exists.
- **P2 — Cross-pollination (owner-answered 2026-08-23: the queue
  SELF-SELECTS).** "I don't know how to steer, let's just do the
  approach I described — find parallel, advanced fields that have
  something important related to RH and include them, then work
  backwards to re-derive fundamentals to the best extent we can."
  So the field-selection criterion is: **the field has something
  important related to the RH** (a declared crosswalk: a theorem,
  technique, or open problem whose statement or proof touches the
  RH / primes / ζ / the critical strip / the zero-free region). The
  queue is GENERATED, not hand-picked: the S12 RH-neighborhood band
  (§3) is the generator — everything the store finds that rides on
  or relates to the RH becomes a candidate field, and each included
  field is trained backwards under the same discipline (declared
  seam lessons + scaffolded derivations, the S8/S9/S10 pattern). A
  field that cannot be expressed on the mechanism is recorded as
  out-of-scope (a wall, not a failure).
- **P3 — Two-tier tracking (owner-answered 2026-08-23, revised
  criteria).** TIER 1 — **the findings log**: ANYTHING the store
  records as a new fact, relationship, or approach at its current
  training level is logged (case-by-case value — "we might have to
  look at it on a case-by-case basis"); if it is true in the store
  it is kept regardless ("if it is 'true' we keep it in case it
  needs to be used for something else" — the store keeps all its
  true facts; the log is the owner's queryable index + a
  case-by-case follow-up flag). TIER 2 — **the breakthrough
  trip-wire**: a lower-layer fact that ALTERS or ADDS TO (or
  falsifies, or recedes a wall of) the layer above is a breakthrough
  candidate — kept even though it may be redundant with tier 1
  ("we absolutely want to know if the level above us is affected so
  we'll keep that, too"). Both tiers live in
  `results/phase-6/fundamentals-breakthroughs.md` (two tables);
  tier-2 candidates never silently absorb — owner rules at band
  close-out (§4).
- **P4 — The owner's directional metric.** The owner's personal
  direction is the track's first compass, not a mandate: the primes
  come from the Sieve of Eratosthenes, the sieve *eliminates*
  (throws away) non-primes, the optimized versions "may have thrown
  away important information", the main ζ "throws away" the
  composites (only the primes appear), and factorization may hide a
  split-tree structure (2·5 vs 5·2). The first bands build backward
  toward exactly this neighborhood; the metric is honored by
  *probing* each claim honestly (the store may confirm a difference,
  or prove a redundancy — both outcomes are lessons).
- **P5 — Definition-layer first.** Cheap invariants (what a thing
  IS, its bookkeeping, its finite censuses) are probed BEFORE
  analytic technique. The owner's hope — "the answer was staring us
  in the face, not something that needed a superior analysis" — is
  encoded as a rule: a band that skips the definitional reconstruction
  and goes straight to the technique is out of pattern.

### H1 — The FULL sieve vs. the optimized sieve (P4: "double work")

The full process: to test n, check whether n can be divided by **any**
k that came before it (2, 3, 4, …, n−1) — composites included. The
optimized sieve skips the composite trials.

- Pre-registered forecast (to be confirmed OR refuted in-store):
  the primality CLASSIFICATION is provably invariant — the FIRST
  divisor in natural order that divides n is always prime (a
  composite trial k of n has a prime factor q < k with q | n, so q
  comes first). Hand-check on 2..200: zero counterexamples. If the
  store PROVES it: a scaffolded theorem (parts: the composite and
  first-divisor facts) — a "confirmed negative" for classification,
  exactly the erdosguess-shape honesty (declared → tested → settled).
- The information that may SURVIVE (the owner's "important
  information"): the bookkeeping. The full process's *kill trace*
