## 2. B-5 CORE — PROVEN in Lean (the remaining 5 real algebra gaps)

On stable, E7a/B-4/B-0 built immediately; B-5's remaining failures were
**not** toolchain regressions but real proof gaps (they appeared
identically on both toolchains). Fixed, each with the documented
justification:

1. `hDApos`/`hDBpos` (`0 < ¼±δ+δ²+γ²`): `nlinarith` (a) does NOT
   zeta-reduce local lets — the goal `0 < DA` is left let-bound, and
   (b) cannot complete the square on its own. Fix: `dsimp only [DA]`
   first (by-name let reduction — the established working pattern),
   then the explicit identity `¼±δ+δ² = (δ±½)²` by `ring` +
   `sq_nonneg`.
2. `hsumOff` (Σ ρ⁻¹ = ω_δ + Bv): two elaboration traps.
   (a) `(((1+2δ)/DA) : ℂ)` elaborates `/` as **complex division of
   per-term-coerced reals** — NOT definitionally
   `ofReal ((1+2δ)/DA)` — which silently defeats later `rw`/`change`
   matching (this was the root of the rc-era spiral, previously
   misdiagnosed as "let opacity"). Fix: hinvA/hinvB targets written
   explicitly `ofReal ((…) / DA)`.
   (b) `field_simp` leaves `.re` projection atoms that `ring` cannot
   see through. Fix: prove hsumOff ENTIRELY in the ofReal image of ℝ:
   `rw [omegaD, Bv]`, `dsimp [DA, DB]` (zeta by name),
   `simp [ofReal_add, ofReal_sub, ofReal_neg]` — closes with no
   projections.
3. hinvA/hinvB last step: `field_simp [hDAz]` does not clear
   `(DA : ℂ)·((DA : ℂ))⁻¹` from the REAL `DA ≠ 0` (the coerced ne is a
   different term). Fix: bridge via `ofReal_eq_zero`
   (`hDAc : (DA : ℂ) ≠ 0`), then `simp [mul_inv_cancel₀ hDAc]` —
   `mul_inv_cancel₀ (h : a ≠ 0) : a·a⁻¹ = 1`,
   Mathlib `Algebra/GroupWithZero/Defs.lean:239` (the plain
   `mul_inv_cancel` in 4.33.1 is the Group/Units variant — wrong
   signature). Note: `ofReal_div` (`Data/Complex/Basic.lean:733`) is
   NOT a simp lemma.
4. `omegaD`/`pref` def bodies: flattened to monomial form
   (¼±δ+δ²+γ²). Mathematical identity ((½±δ)²+γ²), but the OPAQUE `halfR`
   def constant inside `(halfR±δ)²` blocks `ring` from recognizing the
   two forms equal — the `hpref`/`hsumOff` residuals are identical
   real expressions that `ring` only closes when spelled identically.
5. `b5PrefSign`: its sign facts were built in the (halfR±δ)²+γ² form;
   after (4) the flat denominator of `pref` needs the flat sign facts —
   rebuilt all of them in flat form (same square-completion pattern).
