Owner's question of the day (verbatim intent): *"the mirror rotations each

## 1. The mirror scale (answer to the question) — scripts/rh/day003_mirror_scaling.py

**(M1) On the critical line the mirror is pure phase** — `|chi(1/2+it)| = 1` to
≤ 2.3e-10 out to t = 10⁶ (DERIVED identity `1+2sinh² = cosh`, measured on grid).
There is no scale at all on the line — that is exactly why the Riemann–Siegel
phase rotation works there.

**(M2) Off the line the scale is not 1** (MEASURED grid, log form):

|---|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|

- Reciprocal identity `|chi(s)|·|chi(1−s)| = 1` holds to 2.05e-12 (derived, measured).
- Smallest `||chi|−1|` anywhere off-line on the 72-pt grid: **1.144e-2**:
  the line is the ONLY isometric locus on the grid **[CONJECTURE, measured]**.
- Dynamics reading: `|zeta(Ms)| = |zeta(s)|/|chi(s)|` — a near-zero in the
  σ<½ half-plane (where |chi|<1) gets **amplified** by the mirror step. The
  owner's "scale down" is real as near-zero dynamics, but it is a **one-shot
  rescaling**, not an accumulation.

**(M3) There is no shrinking chain** (MEASURED): the mirror group is Klein four;
each mirror is an **involution** (M² = id). At the leak point s0 = iπ/ln2:
M(M(s0)) = s0 exactly; `|chi(s0)| = 0.8493`, `|chi(M s0)| = 1.1774`, product 1.
The orbit closes in ≤ 2 steps — no scale factor can accumulate because the
completed function (not the raw mirror) is the self-reciprocal object.

**(M4) Who leaves the mirror slot empty — factorization decomposition** (MEASURED):

| | at s0 = iπ/ln2 | at mirror M(s0) |
|---|---|---|
| finite factor (1+2^{−s}) | 3.2e-16 (EXACT zero: 2^{−s0} = e^{−iπ} = −1) | 0.5000 (alive) |
| prime part β(s) | 1.438708 (finite) | **1.138511 (O(1), not close to 0)** |
| product = |L12| | 0 | 0.569255 (= day-002 measured value ✓) |

**Conclusion**: the empty mirror slot is empty because the **prime (Euler) part
is O(1) there** — the finite factor `2` broke the orbit, and no zero of the prime
part arrives to fill the slot. *The gap is a property of the factorization, as the
owner suspected* (label: measured, for this finite-factor instance; the open
question is whether any L-function built on a bigger Euler part has a prime part
that can vanish at a finite-factor mirror slot without vanishing at the original
point).

## 2. New machinery: a Riemann–Siegel engine (scripts/rh/zeta_core.py)

`Z_rs(t)`: 2-term RS (`N₀ = ⌊√(t/2π)⌋` — the first version summed to t/2π and
was wrong; corrected), `u = (t/2π)^{1/4}`, remainder `Ψ(p)/u − Ψ‴(p)/(96π²u³)`
with `Ψ(z) = cos(2π(z²−z−1/16))/cos(2πz)`.

Validation (all cross-engine, in-container mpmath):
- vs EM engine (host): t = 45374.03: 2.7e-5; 50000: 1.2e-5; 100000: 5.2e-6 (asymptotic `~u^{−5}` ✓).
- Z_EM vs mpmath dps-30 at those t: **4.4e-11..4.9e-11** — the EM engine (with
  `_NMAX` extended to 80000; the old 9500 cap was the day-3 crash) is exact there.

**Time-budget lesson (the 1000 s incident)**: the v1 census design did 48 RS
bisections + 42 EM bisections per zero → measured 68.7 ms/zero × 128k zeros ≈
76 min, hung past any timeout. Fix: **measure per-evaluation cost on the host
before scaling** (today: Z_rs = 65 µs, Z_EM = 1.56 ms at t = 1e5) and design from
the measured budget: 16 RS bisections + **one** EM check per zero (fallback
bisection only when |Z_EM(tc)| ≥ 1e-4). Final run: **4m33s**, timeout 750s ≈ 2.7×
measured budget.

### The hidden-twin discovery (why v1 of the census was 20 short)

Pass 1 (no dip check) gave N = 138,047 → S(1e⁵) = **−20.56, impossible**
(>30 GUE-σ). Cause: **twin zero pairs with gap < 0.05 inside one walk step**
leave a +/−/+ signature — no net sign change, the inner zero is invisible to a
sign-walk. The per-5000-block deficit map proved the misses were spread
(−1…+4.5) rather than a single hole. Fix: **midpoint dip check** on every
no-flip interval (one extra RS evaluation; sub-walk at dt = 0.0125 when flagged):
**18 hidden twins recovered** → S = −2.56, all block deficits ≤ 2.2 (S-fluctuation
scale). GOE level repulsion (p(s) ∝ s²) predicts ~40 twin pairs with gap < 0.05 in
the range: 23 visible (smallest 0.0208 at t ≈ 63137) + 18 hidden ≈ 41 ✓ consistent.
**Honest residual limitation**: twin pairs with gap < ~0.0125 sitting entirely on
one side of the interval midpoint stay invisible (expected ≲ 0.3 here; the observed
minimum gap 0.0208 suggests none were missed).

### Independent verification of the count (the 2K argument identity)

`S(T) = Arg_cont(ζ)/π − 1`, `N = main + S`, `Arg_cont = ph* + 2πK` with ph* the
principal argument ⇒ **2K = (N − main + 1) − ph*/π must be an even integer.**
Convention sanity at T = 20 (N = 1): 2K = 2 ✓. At T = 1e⁵ with mpmath dps-40:
ph* = 1.3872666648 rad, **2K = −2.000000 ± 6.6e-8 → CONSISTENT** (out_day003_s_arg_mpmath.txt).
The census count is confirmed by a single, completely independent dps-40 evaluation.
