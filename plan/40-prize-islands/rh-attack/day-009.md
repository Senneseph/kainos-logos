## 2. The E7b zero-side kernel — DERIVED (closed form, numerically verified)
From the 25.2.12 product, the canonical per-zero factor is
  E(s/ρ) = (1−s/ρ)·e^{s/ρ},   RH lift: 1−s/ρ = i(γ−t)/(½+iγ).
On-line (s = ½+it), per list-entry γ (both conjugates included):
  |E|²_γ = (t²+γ²)² e^{(tγ+¼)/(¼+γ²)} / (¼+γ²)
  E_γ = i(γ−t)/(½+iγ) · e^{(¼+tγ)/(¼+γ²)}   (+ its conjugate)
  phase_γ(t) = (π/2 − atan2(γ,½)) + (t−γ)/(2(¼+γ²))  [+ π lift at t = γ]
**Verified component-by-component (day009_e7b1_f/d, dps-50):** the
+γ-factor's arg slope vs t = (t−γ)/(2(¼+γ²)) to 1e-12; the jump at a
zero is exactly π (the 2K convention); the full-product arg slope,
zero-free, matches mpmath ζ to 5e-7 rad/rad at t = 14000.

**Route b (Riemann-1859 x-space Abel transform) is DIVERGENT:**
the zero-density kernel x^{−½}cos(γ ln x)/ln x has an Abel transform
~ 2N^{1/2}/ln N (does not decay). The zero-side content of the ζ tail is
NOT the 1859 x-space kernel in the strip; the "+ smooth" remainder of the
1859 kernel is the part that carries it. Recorded as a finding, not a
defect to be patched. (FORMULAS §3.5.)
