# 02 — Scope rulings (owner's filter, applied per target)

The owner's filter: outstanding, **≥ $1,000,000**, and **"requiring
the type of analysis our tool is doing."**

## What "the type of analysis our tool is doing" is (the ruling basis)

The store's analysis register, as of the S13 close-out (1388
assertions, line 0..84), is:

1. **Exact arithmetic, finite scope** — rational/integer values, no
   floating point; every quantity is a member or a quotient of
   members of the line, or an exact product/sum over a *finite*
   index set (the line partial products, the sieve census).
2. **Prime multiplicative structure** — spf chains, valuations
   (latent: v_p is readable off the spf chain), pi-numps, the
   ledger (recorded occurrence, never optimized — S11 policy).
3. **L-function-style finite products** — the zeta family (S13):
   Euler factors (1 ± n^{−s}) over chosen index splits (all /
   primes / composites), partial products at the line end,
   partition identities, telescoping, removal rules.
4. **Scaffolded theorem derivation with strict part-sets** —
   prescribed frontier theorems composed from taught premises,
   premise-checked, exact-arithmetic-verified, failure-honest (the
   nine S4–S13 theorems' contract, owner-accepted framing:
   *verified and filed a prescribed theorem from its own premises,
   or honestly refused*).
5. **Honest walls with named values and named line ends** — the
   store refuses, and says exactly where it stops (the "at the line
   end 84" pattern).

A prize target is **in scope** if its descent-to-fundamentals ends
on this register (the island's destination is *reasoning about and
answering questions about* the problem on the store's terms — the
RH contract: hold the frontier, articulate the missing ingredient),
not merely if it is "number theory."

## The connect requirement (owner: "everything should connect from some fundamental relationships")

The four in-scope islands all rest on the same ground, and the
planning documents name the joints explicitly:

- **RH ↔ BSD:** the L-function soil — zeta's Euler product (built,
  S13) and L(E)'s Euler product (BSD band 1) are siblings in the
  same u-potential family; S13's region/scale/route/relevance
  concepts are the template for reading L(E,1) at the line end.
- **RH/Beal ↔ the spf/ledger soil (S11):** valuation = reading the
  spf chain (Beal); zero-density territory (S13) is the analytic
  twin of the Beal finiteness wall (Darmon–Granville).
- **Beal ↔ CRYPTO (R0–R4):** the v_p + CRT machinery Beal builds is
  the soil the RSA mechanics bands need (R1 modular pairs, R2 the
  structure of (Z/pqZ)^*).
- **P/NP ↔ CRYPTO:** the difficulty-class epistemics (the barriers,
  the first difficulty conjecture) are the same register; R4 *is* a
  difficulty instance the store attempts on its own line.
